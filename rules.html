RULES

OFFICIAL CHENNAI BEYBLADE ASSOCIATION RULEBOOK

🏆 DECK RULES (3 vs 3)

  • • Each player must prepare a fixed deck of 3 Beyblade X combinations.
  • • Blades, Ratchets and Bits may be changed between matches.
  • • No repeating parts across the deck (Blade / Ratchet / Bit).
  • • Deck must be submitted to the referee before the match.
  • • During reshuffle, players may shuffle their Beyblades and return them to the referee before play resumes.

⚔ MATCH RULES

  • • Match continues until a player reaches 7 total points.
  • • Players choose one Beyblade from their deck for each round.
  • • Once used, a Beyblade cannot be reused until all 3 Beyblades are used.
  • • After all 3 Beyblades are used, the deck resets.

🎯 SCORING SYSTEM

Finish Type Points
Spin Finish 1
Over Finish 2
Burst Finish 2
Xtreme Finish 3
Stadium Out 1

🚫 ILLEGAL PLAYS

  • • Launching before referee call.
  • • Touching stadium during battle.
  • • Touching Beyblade during battle.
  • • Stadium tampering.
  • • Unsportsmanlike conduct.

Referee decision is final.

📌 GENERAL RULES

  • • Official Beyblade X stadium only.
  • • Official Takara Tomy & Hasbro Beyblade X parts only.
  • • Modifications are not allowed during the match.
  • • Judges may inspect decks at any time.
